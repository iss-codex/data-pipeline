Data anomalies
=============
CODEX has 2 in-flight issues that affect (increase) the straylight.

1: Earthshine: CODEX mounted on the ISS, at a 51.6 deg inclination relative to the Earth's equator. CODEX track the sun throughout each orbit, typically ~50 minutes of observaitng time out of every 90 minute orbit.
This has the affect of observations being closer to the Earth's limb near sunrise and sunset, and more zenith pointing at middle of observing pass. CODEX sees significant straylgith caused by Earthshine entering the aperture at oblique angles.

2: External occulter to pylon interface: CODEX contains 3 pylons to support eh external occulter. There was a fabrication issue with the pylons, that has resulted in significantly increased starylight around each pylon. This area was deemed non-recoverable, and exposure times were adjsuted to optimize the remaining area. Hence, in a typical exposure the pylons are saturated.
